# Direction Contract

## THESIS

Industry Topic Radar is an evidence-led editorial operations surface for Hangzhou home-improvement agency operators. It makes topic judgment feel like a field inspection rather than a generic analytics dashboard.

## OWN-WORLD

The interface uses the visual language of a site inspection ledger: warm paper, near-black ink, one-pixel rules, numbered evidence photographs, tabular measurements, a restrained review red, and a source-confidence green. Decorative gradients, glass effects, nested cards, and marketing-page composition are excluded.

## STORY

The operator enters with one merchant selected, checks source coverage, reads the strongest market signals, reviews five evidence-backed opportunities, resolves the queue, and unlocks the weekly delivery. Every screen state reinforces that evidence precedes recommendation and human approval precedes delivery.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

At desktop width, the first viewport shows the active merchant, weekly inspection title, five-opportunity status, evidence totals, source coverage, and direct review action. It demonstrates the product mechanism without a marketing hero or feature explanation.

## PROMISE AUDIT

- Evidence is visible before each recommendation.
- Approval is explicit and never implied by generation.
- Demonstration values are labeled as sample data.
- Missing integrations are disabled rather than presented as working.
- No traffic, engagement, lead, or sales outcomes are claimed.
