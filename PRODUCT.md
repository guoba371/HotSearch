# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, selected by the user for the first SaaS surface.

## Users

The primary user is an operator at a Hangzhou content agency managing local home-improvement merchants. They repeatedly research, review, and deliver weekly content opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Industry Topic Radar turns public evidence into five reviewed Core Opportunities per merchant each week. It succeeds when operators save at least three hours of planning time and renew after a paid pilot.

## Positioning

Every delivered opportunity is tied to timely Public Evidence, scored for commercial usefulness, adapted for three channels, and approved by a human reviewer.

## Operating Context

Operators monitor five keywords and up to six competitors for one merchant, review an Editorial Draft on Sunday, and email an approved Weekly Brief on Monday morning.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- V1 includes Radar Profile input, collection visibility, candidate scoring, human review, a read-only Weekly Brief, and email delivery status.
- V1 uses only public sources and never bypasses authentication or platform restrictions.
- V1 does not auto-publish, auto-comment, auto-message, generate finished content, integrate Enterprise WeChat, or promise traffic and sales outcomes.
- The product language in `CONTEXT.md` is canonical.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/v1-plan.md` contains the agreed product and pilot plan.
- `CONTEXT.md` contains the domain glossary.
- No customer testimonials, case studies, logos, or production performance data exist yet and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before recommendation
- Human approval before delivery
- Operational clarity over decorative presentation
- Declare source coverage and gaps honestly
- Validate paid demand before expanding the backend

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain keyboard accessible, readable without color alone, and responsive for desktop and mobile web.
