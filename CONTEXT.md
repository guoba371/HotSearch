# Industry Topic Radar

This context describes a topic-research product for agencies serving local home-improvement businesses in Hangzhou. It separates the paying operator, the businesses they manage, raw topic candidates, and the reviewed output sold to the operator.

## Language

**Operator**:
An agency that pays for and uses the radar on behalf of one or more merchants.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, client

**Merchant**:
A Hangzhou home-improvement business whose market, competitors, and content needs are researched by an operator.
_Avoid_: Account, brand, customer

**Radar Profile**:
The industry, city, keywords, and competitors that define the research scope for one merchant.
_Avoid_: Form, configuration, account

**Topic Candidate**:
A potential content subject discovered from public evidence before scoring and human review.
_Avoid_: Hot topic, idea

**Core Opportunity**:
A scored Topic Candidate supported by multiple pieces of timely Public Evidence and approved by a human reviewer.
_Avoid_: Hot topic, recommendation

**Channel Adaptation**:
An execution of one Core Opportunity shaped for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, or a WeChat public account without changing the underlying subject.
_Avoid_: Topic, duplicate

**Weekly Brief**:
Five reviewed Core Opportunities and their Channel Adaptations delivered to an operator for one merchant.
_Avoid_: Report, dashboard, digest

**Editorial Draft**:
A machine-generated version of a Weekly Brief that cannot be delivered until a human reviewer approves it.
_Avoid_: Weekly Brief, final report

**Public Evidence**:
Publicly accessible material that supports a Topic Candidate without bypassing authentication or platform restrictions.
_Avoid_: Scraped data, platform data

**Evidence Set**:
At least two independent pieces of Public Evidence supporting one Core Opportunity, including at least one item from the preceding seven days.
_Avoid_: Sources, references

**Coverage Gap**:
The declared absence or insufficiency of Public Evidence from an expected source during a Weekly Brief period.
_Avoid_: Missing data, failure
